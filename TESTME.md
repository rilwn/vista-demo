# Vista Operations bilingual interface

1. Open Vista Operations and select **BG** in the top-right of the sign-in page.
   - The sign-in form changes to Bulgarian.
2. Refresh the page.
   - Bulgarian remains selected.
3. Sign in and find **EN / BG** beside the notification bell.
4. Check the sidebar, account menu, Overview page, global search and notification panel.
   - Their labels and messages change with the selected language.
5. From the sidebar choose **ERP → Сервиз**, then open **История на оборудването**.
   - The Service header, tabs, equipment picker, record count, statuses and dates use Bulgarian.
6. Open **Снабдяване → Поръчки за покупка**, then open **Нова поръчка**.
   - The register, form sections, fields, buttons, empty states and validation guidance use Bulgarian.
7. Open **Клиенти и CRM**, then check the partner register, documents and product categories.
8. With an administrator account, open **Сигурност**, **Системна активност** and **Бизнес структура**.
   - Navigation, registers, forms, panels, statuses and pagination use Bulgarian.
9. Open **Справки** and check report filters, saved views, exports and dashboard labels.
10. Select **EN**.
   - The same areas return to English and remain English after refresh.

## Extended workflow check

1. Select **BG** beside the notification bell.
2. From **ERP**, open each area and its tabs: **Снабдяване**, **Финанси**, **Склад**, **Продажби** and **Логистика**.
3. In each area, open one available row and one create action.
   - Headings, tables, forms, panels, statuses, empty states and validation guidance appear in Bulgarian.
4. From **Клиенти и CRM**, open **Хронология**, **Сигнали**, **Анализи**, **Потенциални клиенти** and **Грижа за клиента**.
5. Open one available record or create panel in each area.
   - Interface copy appears in Bulgarian. Customer names, product names, document numbers and entered values remain unchanged.
6. Select **EN**.
   - The same open page and panel return to English without reloading.
